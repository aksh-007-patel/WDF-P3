FAQ (Help Center)

Frequently Asked Questions
Q1. How do I log in to the Student Portal?

Answer: Enter your username and password, then click the Login button.
Q2. How can I reset my password?

Answer: Click on the Forgot Password link and follow the instructions.
Q3. How do I check my attendance?

Answer: Login and open the Attendance page from the Dashboard.
Q4. How do I view my results?

Answer: Click on the Results section in the Dashboard.
Q5. How can I contact the college?

Answer: Go to the Contact Us page or email the college support team.
